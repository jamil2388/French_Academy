# Week 1 — French Foundations

**Goal:** Learn the French alphabet, basic pronunciation, greetings, and polite expressions.

## Daily checklist

### Day 1 — Alphabet and sounds
- [ ] Learn the French alphabet and letter names.
- [ ] Practise the French accented letters: é, è, ê, à, ç, ù.
- [ ] Listen to the alphabet pronounced by a native speaker.
- [ ] Say the alphabet aloud twice.
- [ ] Practise spelling your first and last name in French.

### Day 2 — Greetings and introductions
- [ ] Learn: Bonjour, Bonsoir, Salut, Au revoir, À bientôt.
- [ ] Learn: Comment ça va ? / Ça va bien, merci.
- [ ] Learn: Je m'appelle...
- [ ] Practise a short greeting and introduction aloud.
- [ ] Review Day 1 pronunciation.

### Day 3 — Polite expressions
- [ ] Learn: S'il vous plaît, Merci, Merci beaucoup, De rien.
- [ ] Learn: Excusez-moi, Pardon, Je suis désolé(e).
- [ ] Practise choosing between *tu* and *vous* in simple situations.
- [ ] Create and say five short polite exchanges.
- [ ] Review greetings without looking at your notes.

### Day 4 — Pronunciation practice
- [ ] Practise French vowel sounds with examples.
- [ ] Practise the difference between *u* and *ou*.
- [ ] Practise French nasal sounds with examples.
- [ ] Listen to a short beginner pronunciation lesson.
- [ ] Read 10 familiar French words aloud and imitate the audio.

### Day 5 — Essential classroom phrases
- [ ] Learn: Je ne comprends pas.
- [ ] Learn: Pouvez-vous répéter ?
- [ ] Learn: Parlez lentement, s'il vous plaît.
- [ ] Learn: Comment dit-on ... en français ?
- [ ] Practise a short conversation using these phrases.

### Day 6 — First mini-conversation
- [ ] Introduce yourself using *Bonjour* and *Je m'appelle...*
- [ ] Ask and answer *Comment ça va ?*
- [ ] Say *Enchanté(e)* and *Au revoir* appropriately.
- [ ] Record yourself performing the conversation.
- [ ] Listen to your recording and note sounds to practise again.

### Day 7 — Review and self-check
- [ ] Recite the French alphabet without help.
- [ ] Spell your name aloud in French.
- [ ] Recall at least 10 greetings and polite expressions.
- [ ] Perform a short self-introduction without reading.
- [ ] Review any pronunciation or vocabulary you found difficult.

## Week 1 completion
- [ ] I can recognise and say the French alphabet.
- [ ] I can spell my name in French.
- [ ] I can greet someone and say goodbye.
- [ ] I can introduce myself with a simple sentence.
- [ ] I can use basic polite expressions.
- [ ] I can ask someone to repeat or speak more slowly.
- [ ] I have completed at least one short speaking recording.

**Suggested daily time:** Up to 60 minutes. If you miss a day, continue from the next unchecked item rather than trying to cram.
